Browser setup
The Browser Companion captures your browser downloads and sends them straight to Loadout. It's a one-time, 1-minute setup — you won't need to do this again.
1
Open your browser's extensions page
chrome://extensions
2
Turn on Developer mode
Toggle switch, top-right corner of that page
3
Open the companion's folder
Where it lives on your computer
4
Click "Load unpacked", then pick that folder
The companion will show up in your extensions list, ready to go
📌 Tip: pin it to your toolbar (the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome) so it's always one click away.
